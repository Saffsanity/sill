# `arrow-scrub`

**Checks:** how `ArrowScrub` turns the full key row's `< v ^ >` arrow caps (and `UITextInput`'s Space-bar floating cursor) into a continuous 2D haptic cursor trackpad without giving up single taps or stationary Mac key-repeat: a quick tap under `dragThreshold` (`8 pt`) before `holdDelay` (`0.18 s`) emits one press of that arrow and spends any latched modifiers; a stationary hold past `holdDelay` emits `KeyChord.down` and spends the latch once (never clearing a new modifier armed before lift), releasing cleanly on `end` or `cancel` (or releasing the held chord and immediately quantizing the crossing drag if the finger slides past `dragThreshold`); `end(at:time:)` processes any final finger travel arriving with `touchesEnded` so fast upward flicks never fire a wrong arrow tap; sliding past `dragThreshold` emits ←/→ steps with three-tier velocity acceleration (`8 / 5 / 2.8 pt` at `< 240 / ≥ 240 / ≥ 520 pt/s`, guarded by a 120 Hz `minSampleInterval` floor) and ↑/↓ steps (`16 pt`) with cross-axis delta + remainder dampening, caps combined steps per update to `maxStepsPerUpdate` (`8`), carries latched modifiers on both horizontal and vertical steps, spends an unspent latch if a scrub is cancelled after emitting steps, and leaves zero stuck keys or modifier flags in `KeyStrokes` across fixed scenarios and 3,000 seeded random sessions.

**Compiles:** `iOSClient/ArrowScrub.swift`, `iOSClient/KeyChords.swift`, `iOSClient/TextComposition.swift` and `Sources/SillHost/KeyStrokes.swift` with `Sources/StreamProtocol/*.swift` (`module.py`, `-package-name sill`)
