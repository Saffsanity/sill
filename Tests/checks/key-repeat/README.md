# `key-repeat`

**Checks:** a device's key held down repeats on the Mac as the Mac's own keyboard repeats a key (#162): after the Mac's Delay Until Repeat and at its Key Repeat Rate as NSEvent gives them (Off and nonsense repeat nothing, settings under the floor take it), every key but the modifiers' own, Caps Lock, fn and the media keys, the last key pressed alone (another key's down, text or a gesture's shortcut stops it, an earlier key never takes over again), a modifier pressed or let go mid-hold carried by the repeats after it, stopped at its up (any device's), its device leaving, the host's end and input with nowhere to land, waiting while input is held for an activation and while its device is quiet half a second, then starting over after the delay, its first repeat only once its device is heard no earlier than 0.3 s before it (`KeyRepeat.firstWord`: a letter typed whose up a stall holds back, at every phase of the pings and every stall, is never repeated; a late ping makes it wait; any word since the press for a delay under 0.3 s), never a burst after a late wake; through a model of InputInjector's glue with the real KeyStrokes, the exact repeats and their times, then letters as a device now sends them (P held, P then E, ⇧A with ⇧ let go, A with Caps Lock) and what the window lists say of it (`KeyRepeat.generation`, `keyRepeat` 1), then 2,400 random sessions (600 with nothing in the way, held to the exact cadence) against the invariants

**Compiles:** `Sources/SillHost/KeyRepeat.swift` and `KeyStrokes.swift` with `Sources/StreamProtocol` (`build.sh`, `-package-name sill`)
