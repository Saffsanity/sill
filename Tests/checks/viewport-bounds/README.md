# `viewport-bounds`

**Checks:** what a host takes of a device's viewport (kind 9; `ViewportBounds`, the security scan's finding 4): each side 1 to 16384 points and a text scale 0.25 to 4 or none, any other refused whole, every field out of its range named in the host's line ("width 1e+300 (1 to 16384 points)"); the devices' own panels (Sill.log's, the iPhone Duo's, Larger Picture's room, a Stage Manager window) all taken, the rate no part of it; the bug before it in a copy of the host's fit (the scan's 1e300, a scale of 1e-300, past Int.max: its guard took them and its line's Int() trapped), each refused now; what JSON carries (`NaN`, `Infinity`, `1e999`, a rate of 1e300 do not decode, which the host refuses too); what a device sends (`held`: held to the bounds, a scale that is not a positive number as none, nothing for a side that is not one); the numbers the host's lines print (`whole`, as Int() did toward zero, and `number`, as it came, neither trapping); then 200,000 random viewports, real and hostile: the rule written another way agrees, none the old guard took and its line trapped on is taken now, every viewport taken gives the fit numbers its line prints, whatever a device sends is taken; and a device's late-sound count (`ClientStats.lateSince`, found in the review: the host's stats line added each report's `audioLate` with a plain `+`, and two reports of Int.max within its 1.5 s trapped), a negative or missing count adding nothing and the sum stopping at Int.max, against overflow-reporting arithmetic in 50,000 random runs of reports

**Compiles:** `Sources/StreamProtocol/Viewport.swift` with `StreamMessage.swift`
