# `fence`

**Checks:** the session's fenced hand-overs, hold, adopt, unhold and a new session dropping a hand-over, against a stand-in Mac on loopback: 600 numbered inputs arrive complete and in order, whatever the machine's speed (each step waits for what it needs, not a set time); the inputs counted for the session's connection, which the Mac's pointer reports are judged by, equal those the stand-in read there (every mode, and `count` step by step); the move home from the remote door's TLS to the home door's (TLS since pairing at home), fenced, and with the remote connection gone mid-move; the uplink ledger (`camera`: `UplinkLedger` on its own, its lanes, a connection's generation, an earlier connection's completions counting nothing, the last second's window; then `SessionLink.sendCamera` step by step: a camera frame goes at once and is refused, never kept, at a fence, a hold or with no connection, `uplink` says the fence and the hold, nothing is left in any lane once everything is taken and the last second's bytes are exactly those handed to the session's connection; what each connection carried, frames and inputs in order)

**Compiles:** `iOSClient/SessionLink.swift`, `Sources/StreamProtocol/*.swift`
