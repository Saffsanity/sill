# `mic-send`

**Checks:** the device's microphone on its way to the Mac (`iOSClient/MicrophoneSender.swift` and `MicrophoneCapture.swift`, docs/microphone-plan.md §3.4, §5.6), on the Mac with no audio device: the epoch's format (AAC-ELD 48 kHz mono, 480 frames, priming 240, about 64 kbps); one message of ten packets a capture buffer, the seq counting packets through the epoch, the segment flag on a segment's first message alone; each stamp the capture time of its first packet's first decoded sample; every packet decoding on the Mac's mono decoder and every click of the tone at its whole second ±2 frames, across a lost buffer too (a clean new segment, the seq going on); 4,096, 1,024 and 19,200-frame buffers (every packet, at most 40 a message); epochs and their wrap; nothing made while stopped or for a buffer without a time; the Mac's limits taking every message at a capture buffer's pace; the capture's tap at 48, 24 and 16 kHz (the format first, then about 3 s of packets at 48 kHz, nothing after the stop); the test tone's click on the second and its level

**Compiles:** `iOSClient/MicrophoneSender.swift` and `MicrophoneCapture.swift` with `Sources/SillAudio` and `Sources/StreamProtocol` (`module.txt`; AVFAudio on the Mac)
