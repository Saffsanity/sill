# `window-restore`

**Checks:** a window Sill sized for a device goes back when it stops streaming (issue #57): `keep` at every edge; each fit's record (the first frame kept; a move since noted by `movedSince`, a resize from any edge or corner being none); what becomes of the window at the stop (gone, full screen, home, settled for an app that applies a size late, resized on the Mac and left, its whole frame back, or its own size where it was moved, kept on its display); the virtual display's way home; the ledger (stopping in window ID order, `take`); every line; the sequences that fit a window and put it back (`fit`: never in full screen, a refusal's nudge put back, an unanswered size or a failed read-back recorded; `stop`; `stageHome`; `Ledger.restore`) and `keepsStreaming` and `coversDisplay` for a window off the list on its way into or out of full screen, against a stand-in window that logs every call. Then a stand-in Mac answering the real sequences, with a copy of the coordinator's glue around them: 5,000 random sessions of picks, viewports, restarts, the catalog's resize check and its looks, the person moving, resizing (from any edge or corner), closing and full-screening windows, apps that clamp, snap, apply a size late, answer it late or fail a read-back, windows that cannot be resized, full-screen writes answered either way, windows off the list on their way into or out of full screen, the virtual display on and off, Aa set to Off by the device whose scale fitted the window (two devices: another's viewport without a scale leaves the fit), the last device leaving and Quit, each window held to where the person expects it

**Compiles:** `Sources/SillHost/WindowRestore.swift` (`build.sh`)
