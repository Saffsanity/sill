# `pen`

**Checks:** an Apple Pencil as a pen (docs/pencil-pressure-plan.md). The Mac's side (§4.1), kind 45's messages as what it posts: the pen coming into range before its first point and leaving at each of its causes (the device's leave, a second after an up with no hover since, the device leaving, the host's end), never for mouse input nor for a hover held still, as Sidecar's Pencil (recorded); the tip as the left button, the pen's own (a repeated down, an up without one, a hover or a leave with the tip down, another device's pen during it; a down while another source holds the button goes); pressure (the floor while the tip is down, none while it is up, full when none was sent) and the Pencil's angles as macOS's tilt, sign and scale; the pointer event a message amounts to for kind 8's rules; which messages open the pointer's settle (none without a sample: a leave moves nothing); what still goes with nowhere to land; 5,000 random sessions of two devices' pens and mouse input against a model: every point between an enter and a leave, every down the pen posted with exactly one up, never a leave with the pen's button down. The device's side (§5.1): a stroke as a pen's or the mouse's (the switch, the Mac's `pencil`), fixed as the tip lands; a force over its maximum as pressure; a callback's readings as moves of 32 samples at most, none lost, each sample's time before its own message's last; the leave, only when a Pencil that can hover is neither touching nor hovering a tenth of a second later, and when the surface goes; the finger strokes a resting palm silences, to their end and for half a second after the tip lifts. Then a hover, a stroke and a leave through both ends

**Compiles:** `Sources/SillHost/PenTablet.swift` and `iOSClient/PenStrokes.swift` with `Sources/StreamProtocol` (`build.sh`, `-package-name sill`)
