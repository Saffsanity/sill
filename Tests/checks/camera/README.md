# `camera`

**Checks:** the device's rules for Share Camera (`iOSClient/CameraPolicy.swift`, docs/camera-plan.md §6.1 and §6.2 with §0's settlement): the row (the switch for a Mac that takes the camera, disabled at its 0, "Update Sill" for a Sill.app from before it, nothing for the CLI or a locked Mac); offering (the switch, the Mac's list, the connection) and capturing over every combination of their inputs (kind 16's eight states, iOS's six conditions, five pressures: 5,120 in all), the pause's order (denied, no camera, too warm, not in front, multitasking, the scanner, busy, then waiting), counting as the person's use while capturing; the format (the demand's size and rate, 1280×720 when the camera has no such format, upright inside it, at most 30 fps, 1280×720 away, the heat's 20 and 15 fps and 1280×720); the bitrate (2.5, 1.2 away, 4 for 1080p, by area and rate, the demand's ceiling, 300 kbit/s at least) and its steps (a third down after a second with a skip, a tenth back every 5 s, a fifth of the target at least, never over it); the keyframes (a new epoch, a demand's at most once a second, after a drop, never otherwise); the chip; every word (the footnote for every state and pause, the chip's and VoiceOver's). Then the send budget (`CameraPacing`: a picture skipped before the encoder over the budget or at a fence, an encoded frame dropped only at a fence, never a frame that needs a dropped one) and its model on a virtual clock: a connection of 1, 2, 5, 20 and 100 Mbit/s carrying the camera, a tap every 0.37 s and a move's fence, against the promises (a tap never waits past the budget and one frame; nothing sent that needs a dropped frame; the picture keeps coming; on a link with room, nothing skipped but around a keyframe or the fence; once the bitrate settles, a tap waits under 0.3 s). The plan's first draft forced a keyframe after each drop; this model showed that falling into a keyframe after every frame on a 5 Mbit/s link.
