# `privacy`

**Checks:** Lock this Mac when the last device leaves (docs/privacy-mode-plan.md, part 1; issues #283 and #275): the constants (a 30 s wait, a lock's 3 s to show in the reading, the half-second tick); rule 10's displays a lock screen could take a password at (`typesAt`, through DesktopDisplays.role's real identities: the Mac's own panel and a monitor count, Sill's stage and Desktop displays and macOS's stand-in for a Mac with no screen, #298's, do not); the switch off (nothing ever happens or is said); which departures count and the wait (the last session's departure with the switch on starts it, one registering ends it, two devices and a move, zero twice, a count below zero, the switch turned off, turned on with no device or after a departure, its boundary at exactly 30 s, a test host's shorter wait in every line); its end (the lock with its line when the Mac reads unlocked with a display; locked already, another user on the screen, a reading not established and no display each say why and lock nothing; the displays looked at only then); the confirmation (the reading says locked, off console too; still unlocked at 3 s; the call unavailable, answered once; a device back meanwhile, whose display never sleeps; one lock at a time, a wait that ends under one saying so); the display sleep after the lock and after a failure (#275); the failure Settings keeps until a lock happens, and its warning (off: none; no display before a failure); a quit (its line once, nothing after it, the devices leaving as Sill quits starting no wait) and a crash (a new launch's state locks nothing without a departure it saw); the TEST ONLY hooks (a test host's alone, their bounds and lines); every word; then a model of the server's registrations (sessions, cut off ones as they leave, and connections that never register: a pairing connection, one the device gate refuses, one an update's hold sends away) and the coordinator's glue (ticks exactly while a wait or a confirmation runs, a tick at a reading's change, the call answered later, macOS's lock showing late or never) through 20,000 seeded random sessions against eight invariants: a lock only at a wait's end (the switch on, no session, not quitting, unlocked with a display, between the wait and a tick after it), at most one per departure and none while one is unconfirmed, every lock decided within its 3 s and a tick, the displays asleep only right after an outcome and never with a session, `pending` exactly a wait or a confirmation, every wait that ends on its own saying why as the model expects, nothing in a session whose switch was never on, nothing after a quit
