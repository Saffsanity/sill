# `privacy`

**Checks:** Lock this Mac when the last device leaves (docs/privacy-mode-plan.md, part 1; issues #283 and #275): the constants (a 30 s wait, a lock's 3 s to show in the reading, the half-second tick); who acts (`locksForReal`: Sill.app itself alone, never with `--synthetic`; the CLI, the bare SillMenuBar binary and a test host make the dry run); the gate's reading as the rule reads it (`mac`: a reading not established is unknown, never unlocked); rule 10's displays a lock screen could take a password at (`typesAt`, through DesktopDisplays.role's real identities: the Mac's own panel and a monitor count, Sill's stage and Desktop displays and macOS's stand-in for a Mac with no screen, #298's, do not); the switch off (nothing ever happens or is said); which departures count and the wait (the last session's departure with the switch on starts it, one registering ends it, two devices and a move, zero twice, a count below zero, the switch turned off, turned on with no device or after a departure, its boundary at exactly 30 s, a test host's shorter wait in every line); its end (the lock with its line when the Mac reads unlocked with a display; locked already, another user on the screen, a reading not established and no display each say why and lock nothing; the displays looked at only then); the confirmation (the reading says locked, off console too; still unlocked at 3 s; the call unavailable, answered once; a device back meanwhile, whose display never sleeps; one lock at a time, a wait that ends under one saying so); the display sleep after the lock and after a failure (#275); Turn off its display too off (locked: its own line and no display sleep, a device back or not; a failure still sleeps the display; the switch read when the lock is confirmed); Only after a device away from home (a visit at home alone starts no wait and says so; one session away in the visit is enough, whoever leaves last, and one before a return within the wait; each visit starts afresh, after a wait that ended, a departure with the switch off or the switch turned off during a wait; turned on during a wait it ends one with no session away and keeps one with; turned off it starts none; after a quit or with the switch off it says nothing); the failure Settings keeps until a lock happens, and its warning (off: none; no display before a failure); a quit (its line once, nothing after it, the devices leaving as Sill quits starting no wait) and a crash (a new launch's state locks nothing without a departure it saw); the TEST ONLY hooks (a test host's alone, their bounds and lines); every word (the dry run's, the start line in each form); then the rule driven as the coordinator's glue drives it (each registration's count then its route, home or away; departures; the three switches; the reading; the call answered later; macOS's lock showing late or never; ticks exactly while a wait or a confirmation runs) through 20,000 seeded random sessions against eight invariants: a lock only at a wait's end (the switch on, a session away in the visit when only after one, no session, not quitting, unlocked with a display, between the wait and a tick after it), at most one per departure and none while one is unconfirmed, every lock decided within its 3 s and a tick with the line the reading, the sessions and the display's switch call for, the displays asleep only right after a lock's outcome that calls for it and never with a session, `pending` exactly a wait or a confirmation, every departure, switch and wait's end saying what the model expects, nothing in a session whose switch was never on, nothing after a quit. Which connections register at all (never a pairing connection, one the device gate refuses or one an update's hold sends away; one cut off leaves like any other) is StreamServer's, outside this check: `clients` changes only in its `register` and `unregister`, and Scripts/privacy-check/live.py runs the pairing and the gate cases live
