# `ledger`

**Checks:** the device's settings ledger against a model host, scenarios and 5,000 random runs (some against a host without sound: Send Audio never sent there; some against a host from the sound's beta, which refuses a device's Send Audio: the switch goes back; some against a host from before 2026-10-29, which takes Direct Wireless from any session). Rule 10 (2026-10-29): Direct Wireless never sent on a session the Mac says is not paired (`sessionPaired` false), on or off, the rest of a pick still sent; sent as before to a Mac that does not say; each connection's own state, devices paired during their session and removed, and a test client's pick refused and answered with the Mac's value. The Virtual Desktop (phase 2b), the eighth field: rule 9 against a host without it, rule 11 (only on a session the Mac says is paired, on or off, never on one that does not say), on at once and settled by its answer, refused where the Mac cannot make it and back, unanswered and back at the timeout; in the random runs a host with it (its state's availability changing), a device's switch disabled while the Mac says it cannot make it, and floors on its picks taken, refused unpaired, held by rule 11 and refused unavailable

**Compiles:** `iOSClient/HostSettingsLedger.swift`, `Sources/StreamProtocol/HostSettings.swift`
