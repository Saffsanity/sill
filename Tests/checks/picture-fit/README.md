# `picture-fit`

**Checks:** where the streamed picture is drawn in its pane (PictureFit, 2026-11-25): aspect-fit in the pane's middle as the video layer's `resizeAspect` draws it, except a picture that is the pane's own pixels less at most two a side, never more, which is drawn one picture pixel on one screen pixel from the pane's top-left (an iPhone's pixel-for-pixel Virtual Desktop, Noah 2026-11-24: an iPhone 15 Pro's 1178×2378 picture in its 1179×2379-pixel pane, the spare pixel a hairline at the right and bottom). The iPhone 15 Pro's layouts by hand (upright and on its side with Full Screen one for one; with its controls, grown to 1297×540 and 866×540 since Noah's probes P3 and P4, fitted as before; a picture one pixel short of its pane), an iPad's exact fill, a window two pixels narrow, the pane's own origin, fractions of a point, no scale, no picture; then 50,000 random panes and pictures against the rule and the old aspect-fit

**Compiles:** `iOSClient/PictureFit.swift`
