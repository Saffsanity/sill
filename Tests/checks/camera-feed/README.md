# `camera-feed`

**Checks:** Sill Camera's rules (`CameraFeedPolicy`, docs/camera-plan.md §6.4), the Core Media I/O camera extension's one reducer: its constants (NV12 `420v` at 1280×720 then 1920×1080, 15 to 30 fps and an app's frame duration read inside them, a frame standing a second, a 60 Hz timer, a sink of two frames, the caps); the state Sill for Mac writes (`off`, `unavailable`, `waiting`, a device's name and pause as JSON: round trips, 4,096 bytes at most, unknown words and empty names refused, a name cleaned of line breaks, control and format characters, at most 40 characters) and the demand it reads (running, size, rate, the apps by identifier and pid: only what Sill Camera could have written, identifiers in printable ASCII); who may feed Sill Camera (Sill for Mac's signing identifier, never another app or none, never a foreign signature, its signature unchecked only where the sandbox would not say, one Sill at a time, the first keeping it); the placeholder's words for each state ("Open Sill on this Mac." while nothing feeds; only a device state names a device); state writes (taken only while Sill feeds; anything but the same device's live camera retires the newest frame); frames (only the feeder's, only while it says a device's camera is live, NV12 of the size apps take; kept while no app runs the stream or more wait behind it, else sent at once); the timer (nothing while no app runs the stream; the newest frame again at the app's rate, with half a tick of slack, while it is under a second old; the placeholder, at once when its words or size change); apps and the demand. Then 20,000 random lives (Sill and other clients starting, stopping and leaving, states written while fed and not, frames of every kind, formats, rates, apps, ticks steady and not) against the invariants after every step: the feeder, the state and the format as the rules say; nothing said and no frame kept while nothing feeds; a picture only while an app runs the stream, from the feeder, while it says that device's camera is live, of the size apps take, under a second old and after anything that retired it; every placeholder's words the state's, a device named only by its own state; never more than four ticks without anything while an app runs the stream; the demand what runs
