# `recording-notice`

**Checks:** what the Mac says of a device recording the stream (#284, docs/recording-plan.md; `RecordingNotice`): its window lists' `recordings` (generation 1), the log's words ("Recording: iPad (iPad14,1) started recording the stream.", "… stopped …", "… left while recording the stream.", and "(3 more changes since the last line)", one change in the singular), and each connection's lines from the device's reports (`ClientStats.recording`): a change written at once, then at most a line every 10 s, the changes in between counted in the next line and the state brought up to date by the first report once the spacing is over (the spacing's edge, a flood of 32 changes in 7 s making two lines, a stop and a start held then said again); as the connection closes, "left while recording" while it records, a stop the log held back, else nothing, and a second close nothing; then 20,000 random sessions (a recording of minutes, of seconds, or a flood), each report checked against the invariants: lines at least 10 s apart, each saying the device's state at its report, every change said or counted exactly once, the log saying what the device does by the first report after the spacing, and the close's line

**Compiles:** `Sources/SillHost/RecordingNotice.swift` (`module.txt`, `-package-name sill`)
