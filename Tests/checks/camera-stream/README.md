# `camera-stream`

**Checks:** the device's camera stream (`iOSClient/CameraStream.swift`, docs/camera-plan.md §6.1 points 4 to 8 and §6.2 with §0's settlement), what becomes of each picture the camera gives and each frame the encoder makes: nothing while stopped; an encoder and a new epoch at the first picture, at a new size (the device turned), a new rate or camera and after a stop, never for a new target bitrate alone (taken at once), at the bitrate its steps reached; the epoch's format right before its first frame, which must be a keyframe with generation 1's parameter sets (no keyframe, none, a NAL length of 3: dropped, a keyframe next, the gap in the numbers); the format's camera, size and bitrate; an older encoder's frames never going; keyframes at an epoch's start, the Mac's ask (once a second at most), after a frame dropped or one the encoder did not give back, never after a skip; the send budget's skips (a fence, a hold, more not taken than the budget) and drops (a fence that went up since the encoding); after a drop nothing but a keyframe goes; a frame past 256 KB dropped and the bitrate a third down at once, one of exactly 256 KB going; an empty one dropped; a keyframe with new parameter sets opening an epoch of its own, the same ones keeping it; epochs going round after 65,535; the bitrate's steps (a third down after a second with a skip, a fifth of the target at least, a tenth back every 5 s, never over the target) and a second's numbers. Then 20,000 random lives (runs and stops, pictures at two sizes over random uplinks, frames back from current and older encoders, fences, frames past the cap, new parameter sets, failures, asks, seconds) against S1 to S10: every frame after its epoch's format; a keyframe right after each format; numbers in order and never twice; nothing of an older encoder; no frame at a fence; only a keyframe after a drop; a keyframe encoded exactly when owed; nothing encoded over the budget; every format valid and every frame on the wire; the bitrate within its bounds

**Compiles:** `iOSClient/CameraPolicy.swift` and `iOSClient/CameraStream.swift` with `Sources/StreamProtocol` (`module.txt`)
