the clipboard chip's widest while the toggle shows (the room beside it on the chip's side, 8 pt clear of it, whichever way the layout runs); every keyboard top against both rules, and every reading the monitor can make never putting the row under the glass or over the picture, nor leaving neither row nor toggle.
