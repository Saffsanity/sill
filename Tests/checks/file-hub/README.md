# `file-hub`

**Checks:** files from devices on the Mac end to end without a host (`FileHub` with `FileStore`, `LockPolicy` and the ledgers; docs/files-plan.md §7 and §11): a device's kinds 34 and 49 fed as StreamServer hands them on, with the lock's reading at their arrival, to connections that are never started, on a scratch folder of its own under `.build/checks/file-hub`, never `~/Downloads`. A transfer of three files (one of 300 KB in 64 KiB parts, one empty, one hidden by name) accepted at once, an ack for each part, `done` with the names as kept and the place, the files byte for byte, quarantined, nothing left in the staging folder, the same name again numbered; refusals in the rule's order with nothing written (an unpaired session, one the coordinator never named, an offer that arrived while the Mac read locked though it is unlocked now, off console, the switch off, an id twice, no room); ten offers at once (one taken, one busy, eight past the rate), a part with no transfer dropped, a part of another total cancelled `damaged`; and every stop mid-transfer deleting its partial and keeping nothing: a part that arrived while locked, the Mac locking, the switch going off, the device removed from the trust list, the device leaving (nothing sent), the device's own cancel, the coordinator no longer listing the session, a quit

**Compiles:** `Sources/SillHost/FileHub.swift`, `FileStore.swift`, `LockPolicy.swift`, `RefusalSummary.swift` and `Stats.swift` with `Sources/StreamProtocol` (`module.txt`, `-package-name sill`)
