# `microphone`

**Checks:** kind 48, the device's microphone as Sill Microphone on the Mac (`Sources/StreamProtocol/Microphone.swift`, docs/microphone-plan.md §4), and both ends' rules (`MicrophonePolicy.swift`, §3.3 with the 2026-11-18 overview's rules). The wire: its three types (type 1 `MicrophoneFormat`, the offer, its claim and pause, an epoch's format, whose JSON is kind 43's `AudioFormat`'s with `claim` and `pause` beside, read by kind 43's reader and reading kind 43's JSON; type 2 kind 43's `AudioPackets` byte for byte, the header's stamp the capture time; type 3 the single byte 3) round-trip; kind 43's caps at their edges (4,096 and 16,384 bytes) and generation 1's bounds (AAC-ELD, 48 kHz, one channel, 480 or 512 frames, priming, epoch, cookie, bitrate); a writer refuses rather than cuts; an epoch's format whole or refused; unknown types, keys, pauses and states read as the rules say; kind 16's `MicrophoneState` and the window list's `microphone`, skipped by older readers; 20,000 random and mutated payloads that never trap, each value read inside the bounds and writing back to what was read. The device: its rule over all 13,824 combinations of its inputs (offers, its pause, records: never in the background, locked, off or from a window not chosen), its link's messages (the claim once, the pause, the end when the switch goes off and once when the Mac says `inUse` to a connection that does not offer, nothing to a Mac locked or without the field), the window it records from, the row's every word. The Mac: its reducer's states, holder, claims and takeovers (the previous holder told), moves and the grace, ends from a session that no longer offers, the successor, refusals (unpaired, a plain door, the switch, readiness, the lock and its lines), the limits at their edges (10 offers a second, 6 epochs a minute, 1.5 s of sound a second over 2 s, an hour at one a second), silence, clean names.
