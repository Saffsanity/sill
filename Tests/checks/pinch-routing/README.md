# `pinch-routing`

**Checks:** who a two-finger pinch belongs to (Pinch, Zoom Picture or Zoom on ‹Mac›; PR #261 and its review of 2026-11-18): the picture's zoom, the Mac's own pinch (`InputEvent.magnify`) or nobody's, from fingers on the picture, a trackpad of Sill's own (the on-screen one, Trackpad mode's over the picture) or one connected to an iPad, and whether the Mac takes the pinch; all twelve combinations, then the properties the callers rely on (the Mac sent a pinch only under Zoom on ‹Mac› and only by a Mac that takes it, the picture zoomed only by fingers on it, Zoom Picture as before #261, one choice for every source, a kept Zoom on ‹Mac› never costing the picture its zoom on an older Mac); the turn (Rotate on ‹Mac›) only with the Mac's pinch; the Mac's `pinch` flag; the two switches as UserDefaults holds them (off unless turned on, their keys, the panel's Bool, a number, a launch argument's string, and `-Sill.pinchZoomsMac 1 -Sill.rotateOnMac 1` in a child run); the Settings panel's rows (the control showing what fingers do, dimmed for an older Mac, Rotate on ‹Mac› only under Zoom on ‹Mac›) and their words

**Compiles:** `iOSClient/PinchRouting.swift`
