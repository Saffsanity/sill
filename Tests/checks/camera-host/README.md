# `camera-host`

**Checks:** the Mac's rules for the devices' cameras (`CameraHostPolicy`, docs/camera-plan.md §6.3), one reducer driven step by step: its constants (a grace of 10 s, 10 formats and 60 frames a second per session, a keyframe a second, a refusal's line a minute, 1280×720 and 1.2 Mbit/s away, the home bitrates by area and rate); who may (the Mac's switch, Sill Camera's readiness, any lock, the trust list: an unpaired session's or a plain door's claim takes nothing, counted and said once a minute, and counts once it pairs); one device: its offer, an app opening Sill Camera (kind 16 `inUse` before the demand, the app's size and rate with a keyframe and a token), an epoch's format making the decoder, frames of it taken and of any other dropped, a new epoch, the app's size changing (a demand without a keyframe), the app closing (idle, no decoder, the old epoch never taken again); pauses (said once, shown by Sill Camera, still wanted), two and three devices (an offer takes nothing from a holder, a claim takes it, a stop hands it to the most recent standing offer); a move (the new connection offers within the grace and is asked with a keyframe), a departure (the grace, then the next device), a removal (at once); the lock (kind 16 without a camera, nothing wanted or shown, frames and offers refused, the holder kept however long, wanted again at the unlock with a keyframe, free if its device says nothing within the grace after); the rates at their edges; a failed decode's keyframe at most once a second; away (fitted into 1280×720, even, at most 30 fps, 1.2 Mbit/s); a device whose hello reads no type 3 never sent one; the switch and readiness while in use; the TEST ONLY hook (`CameraTestHook`: SILL_TEST_CAMERA's `count` and `dump:DIR` only on a test host, one line anywhere else and none when unset, SILL_TEST_CAMERA_DEMAND's steps read, sorted and refused at their edges). Then 20,000 random lives of one to four devices (connects and moves, leaves, offers, claims, pauses, stops, epochs, frames, failed decodes, removals and pairings, locks, the switch, readiness, apps opening and closing Sill Camera, time) against the invariants after every step: a holder always has an offer; one session wanted at most, the holder's, paired, only while the camera is available and an app uses it; nothing shown while unavailable; a claim from a session that may always takes the camera; kind 16 as the receiver keeps it always the rule's; a frame taken only from the wanted session, of its announced epoch; a keyframe asked for a failed decode never within a second of the last; a free camera never left with a standing offer from a session still here

**Compiles:** `Sources/SillHost/CameraHostPolicy.swift` and `CameraTestHook.swift` with `Sources/StreamProtocol` (`module.txt`)
