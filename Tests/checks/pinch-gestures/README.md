# `pinch-gestures`

**Checks:** a device's two-finger pinch and turn (`InputEvent.magnify` and `.rotate`, PR #261 and its review) as the Mac's own trackpad gestures, what InputInjector posts for each (`PinchGestures`): the pointer under a gesture as it begins (only when it is more than a point away, and only for the first of the two to begin), every event of a gesture where it began whatever place a device sends later, a began over an open one of its kind ending that one first, a change or an end with none open posting nothing, each value bounded (a doubling for the magnification, a quarter turn for the turn) and a value or a place that is not a number dropped whole, the magnify and the rotate open together at one place, one ending while the other goes on, and the watchdog (a second of quiet) and the host's end ending what a device left open, with 0, where it is; then 4,000 random sessions through a model of the injector's glue with its watchdog against the invariants (every began ended, in order, at its place, bounded numbers, the pointer moved only as a gesture opens, nothing open after a second of quiet)

**Compiles:** `Sources/SillHost/PinchGestures.swift` with `Sources/StreamProtocol` (`build.sh`, `-package-name sill`)
