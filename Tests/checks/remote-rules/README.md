# `remote-rules`

**Checks:** the Remote rows and automatic remote dial, the order a saved Mac's addresses are tried in, what a failure means, saved Macs (DNS names distinguished from IP ranges, and typed destinations kept at their effective ports across a key change); pairing at home's model, a look-alike under a saved Mac's name among it, and the saved Macs' home fields (`home.swift`); the device key's rules (`DeviceKeyPolicy`) case by case, then a launch, a pairing, a SIGKILL and the next launch as StreamClient and DeviceIdentity chain them, through a stand-in Keychain and UserDefaults with each way either fails (a restore from another device's backup, a key never kept, kept and never found again, a failed read at launch or at a pairing, a lost write, unreadable defaults), and 4,000 random lives against the invariants (`launch.swift`, #140); the dial failure's words where either end's VPN may be off (#309, `RemoteCopy`); the VPN addresses a saved Mac keeps when a later list from it lacks them (`vpnmemory.swift`: Noah's night of 2026-11-11 as a model, the rule's edges (only kind "vpn", 90 days to the second, four, newest first, a clock ahead, the old record's time), the dial's second group, what counts at a remembered address, every failure's words with only remembered addresses to dial, a new pairing, a key change, Forget, the Remote rows, a build from before reading a newer record, and 2,000 random lives against an independent bookkeeping of when each address was last listed)

**Compiles:** `iOSClient/DiscoveryPolicy.swift`, `RemoteDialPolicy.swift`, `SavedMacs.swift` with `Sources/StreamProtocol` (`build.sh`), with `main.swift`, `home.swift`, `launch.swift` and `vpnmemory.swift`
