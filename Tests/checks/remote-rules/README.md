# `remote-rules`

**Checks:** the Remote rows and automatic remote dial, the order a saved Mac's addresses are tried in, what a failure means, saved Macs (DNS names distinguished from IP ranges, and typed destinations kept at their effective ports across a key change); pairing at home's model, a look-alike under a saved Mac's name among it, and the saved Macs' home fields (`home.swift`); the device key's rules (`DeviceKeyPolicy`) case by case, then a launch, a pairing, a SIGKILL and the next launch as StreamClient and DeviceIdentity chain them, through a stand-in Keychain and UserDefaults with each way either fails (a restore from another device's backup, a key never kept, kept and never found again, a failed read at launch or at a pairing, a lost write, unreadable defaults), and 4,000 random lives against the invariants (`launch.swift`, #140); the dial failure's words where either end's VPN may be off (#309, `RemoteCopy`); the VPN addresses a saved Mac keeps when a later list from it lacks them (`vpnmemory.swift`: Noah's night of 2026-11-11 as a model, the rule's edges (only kind "vpn", 90 days to the second, four, newest first, a clock ahead, the old record's time), the dial's second group, what counts at a remembered address, every failure's words with only remembered addresses to dial, a new pairing, a key change, Forget, the Remote rows, a build from before reading a newer record, and 2,000 random lives against an independent bookkeeping of when each address was last listed); an address typed onto a saved Mac's record without pairing (`typedadd.swift`: added as the last typed address, one destination once across the Mac's list, the typed ones and the remembered addresses still within their season, the host's case and its port, never one `keepable` refuses, the cap of eight, another record untouched, the record's encoding, and a kind 18's refresh keeping it; the dial puts the Mac's own list first and the typed ones in a group of their own after it, where another key and a name that no longer resolves count for nothing and no other saved Mac's key is taken)

**Compiles:** `iOSClient/DiscoveryPolicy.swift`, `RemoteDialPolicy.swift`, `SavedMacs.swift` with `Sources/StreamProtocol` (`build.sh`), with `main.swift`, `home.swift`, `launch.swift`, `vpnmemory.swift` and `typedadd.swift`
