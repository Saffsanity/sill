The Sill Film

Sill in a minute and 23 seconds: your Mac’s windows on an iPad, what you can do with them there, and how your iPad reaches your Mac.

What the film shows

The film has music and no voice. Here are its words in the order they appear, each with what the picture shows.

  • Your Mac apps. On the window sill. A Mac, an iPad and a window, drawn as in Sill’s icon.
  • Any Mac window. An iPad connects to a Mac, and the bar along its top shows the Mac’s windows as live thumbnails.
  • One tap to switch. A tap on a thumbnail brings up that window.
  • Or the whole desktop. The Desktop button brings up the Mac’s whole desktop.
  • App drawer from afar. The Apps drawer lists the apps on the Mac.
  • Text at the size you choose. Sliding on Aa resizes the Mac window, and its text grows on the iPad.
  • Click. Scroll. Type. With haptic trackpad. Held upright, the iPad shows the window above a row of keys and a trackpad, and the Mac’s pointer follows a finger on the trackpad. In small type: “Haptics on iPhone.”
  • Close. Minimize. Full Screen. Holding a thumbnail shows the window’s three buttons.
  • Latency you don’t notice. Hardware-encoded HEVC, straight to your iPad.
  • Up to 120 fps. On ProMotion displays. The number rolls up from 60.
  • Hidden on your Mac. Still live on your iPad. A window on a drawn Mac is covered by another, and on the iPad it keeps streaming. In small type: “With Virtual Display on, Sill streams the window from an invisible virtual display.”
  • Wi-Fi. Wired. Direct. Direct needs no shared network. The connect screen lists three Macs, each with how it is reached. In small type: “With Direct Wireless Connection on in Sill.”
  • Or remote, through your own VPN. A fourth Mac joins the list as Remote. In small type: “With Remote Access on in Sill.”
  • Pair once. Remote Access lets in only the devices you pair. The Mac’s pairing window with its code, beside an iPad adding that Mac. Then the iPad’s settings say it is connected through Tailscale.
  • No subscription. No servers.
  • Sill. Free. Open source. For iPhone and iPad, with a free Mac companion. Sill’s icon and name, and in small type a trademark notice.
  • Designed for iPhone Duo. Both screens. Coming soon. A folding iPhone opens.

Get Sill

Sill for Mac is free, for Mac computers with Apple silicon on macOS 14 or later. Sill for iPhone and iPad is on its way to the App Store; until it is there, you can build it from its source code.
