**Follow the Pointer, a switch over the zoomed picture's follow: PR #327 finished (2026-11-18, branch `ready-327` from wedi-dev's `pointer-edge-follow` at 1387e9f, main at eb48ac9 merged in).**
Issue #311 asks for a zoomed picture that follows the pointer. PR #327 (wedi-dev) is the smaller change
Noah invited on #216 (2026-10-29: "Pointer following near the edges is a nice touch; if you'd like to bring
it to the layout we have, I'd be glad to look at it as a smaller change"): a zoomed picture moves just far
enough to keep the upright trackpad's pointer inside a 44 pt band (a quarter of a small pane), stopping at
the picture's edges, and the pad's step is divided by the zoom so the pointer keeps its speed on screen.
The review of 2026-11-18 (~/Downloads/sill-handoff/next/feature-requests/pr-evaluations/touch.md, "#327")
found no defect and asked for a switch, off by default, over both halves, a shared hook, and the words:
today it follows, and slows the pointer, for everyone who zooms, which Noah's #65 decision of 2026-10-19
ruled out ("no cursor-centred zoom and no view following the pointer"), and his rule of 2026-11-18 is a
switch ("Anything that currently exists as a community PR should be reevaluated now ... and of course to
add toggles").
- Kept: wedi-dev's two commits as they are (c14b7e4, the follow, `StreamZoom.followTrackpadPointer` and its

- What was added, and why:
  1. **Follow the Pointer**, this device's switch, off unless turned on (`StreamZoom.FollowPointer`,
     `Sill.followPointer`, never sent): the Settings panel's This iPhone or This iPad, right after Reverse
     Scrolling, the other row about the trackpad and the picture, on every device (the upright pad shows
     wherever a portrait layout does: a phone, the iPhone Duo upright, an iPad's narrow window), with the
     review's words: "While the picture is zoomed, it moves to keep the pointer in view as the pointer
     nears an edge, and the trackpad moves the pointer more slowly the further you zoom in." Off, nothing
     of #327 happens: the pad's step is the same in the frame at every zoom, and the picture moves only
     under two fingers. On, #327 as it was. In draft #393's whole-screen Settings the row comes along
     inside This ‹device› (`devicePart`), after Reverse Scrolling; the review's Touch and Zoom group, when
     it is made, takes both.
  2. **The shared hook.** `TrackpadFollow` (TrackpadView.swift): the picture's zoom (the contributor's
     `readZoom`) and its follow, which the portrait stream screen gives every trackpad on it through the
     environment (`\.trackpadFollow`, on its body); `TrackpadView` hands it to its surface at each update
     (`TrackpadSurface.picture`), and `moveCursor` reads the switch once a move (`FollowPointer.padStep`,
     `follows`) and hands the pointer to the follow only with it on while zoomed. The upright pad's call is
     main's again, and #222's pad over the picture in the portrait layouts takes the same by itself once it
     can zoom (it cannot today: Trackpad mode has no pinch); its landscape pad would need the same line on
     StreamScreen, which has no trackpad today. The environment, rather than more arguments on `Trackpad`
     and `TrackpadView`, keeps this branch off the lines drafts #397, #398 and #411 add their own
     arguments to, so they merge with it as they are.
  3. **The words.** StreamZoom's header records the three dates (#65, #216, 2026-11-18) and the switch;
     the README and the support page say "With Follow the Pointer on"; docs/DEVELOPMENT.md's "Pinch zoom"
     says what it does; `-Sill.followPointer 1` and `-SillSettingsScroll follow` (BUILD-AND-RUN.md,
     DEVELOPMENT.md, ContentView's contract); LAYOUT.md's `StreamZoom` entry.
  4. **The check** (the project's standard): `Tests/checks/stream-zoom` gains 28 checks, 161 in all: the
     switch's default, key and words, how it reads what UserDefaults holds (the panel's Bool, a number, a
     launch argument's string), off and on at 1×, 2× and 5×, and 20,000 random zooms, steps and pointers:
     off, the step and the picture as before #327; on, the pointer as far on screen at any zoom, the zoom
     kept, the pane covered, a pointer inside the band moving nothing, one outside it ending inside it
     unless the picture's edge stops it, following again moving nothing. Mutants F6 to F8 (the band, its
     far side, the picture's edge) and P1 to P11 (the switch). Harness photo cases:
     `Scripts/harness-photos/follow-pointer.cases`.
