**Picture in Picture: the stream stops when it ends in the background, #379 (2026-11-18, branch `pip-lock-379` from main at eb48ac9).**
- The issue (Noah, 2026-11-16): an iPhone locked while Sill showed his Mac in Picture in Picture kept its
  session for about 38 s more, taking frames at a trickle (1 fps, up to 35 s late), every sound packet
  dropped, and the Mac's LinkJudge blamed the link ("cannot carry Extreme… suggesting Low · Standard").
- The cause, by reading: Picture in Picture's end (here `end(.deviceLocked)` on iOS's
  protectedDataWillBecomeUnavailable, about ten seconds after a lock with a passcode) only stopped Picture
  in Picture. `pictureInPictureChanged` then set `appInForeground` false (`runningChanged`) and nothing
  ended or paused the session: Sill was in the background with a live connection and no picture, until iOS
  stopped it. Two things there kept it from being suspended at once, as a plain trip to the background is
  (the Mac then drops a device that drains nothing for 4 s, StreamServer's `deadAfter`): the app's audio
  session, which `holdSession(offered || active)` keeps active while the picture is still offered (the
  hub's choice stands while no window is in front, and the session still qualifies), with
  `UIBackgroundModes` audio; and whatever time iOS gives an app as its Picture in Picture ends. Which of the
  two set the 38 s, and why reading slowed to about a frame a second (a background app's decoder and CPU),
  needs a device's console; the fix does not depend on it. The same goes for every other end of Picture in
  Picture with Sill away: its window's close button, ten minutes paused, a failed start.
- The rule (PictureInPicturePolicy.endsSession, pure, checked): Picture in Picture was on and is off, with
  the app in the background: its window's session ends. Never with Sill in the foreground (the return
  button, Sill opened while it shows), never at a pause (the connection is kept), never a stay in the
  background without it (iOS suspends Sill as before: a short trip keeps its session). Looked at as it ends
  and again `endSettle` (0.5 s) later, and it ends only if both say so and the connection is the same: the
  return button stops Picture in Picture as iOS brings Sill forward, in iOS's order.
- The glue: `StreamClient.endSessionIfAway` (from `pictureInPictureChanged`) and
  `endSessionInBackground` (StreamClient+Remote): the connection closed from this side (`cancel`, a clean
  close: the Mac's "Client left" and, with no other device, the capture stops; no link verdict, as the
  device is gone), the loss's own words ("‹Mac› disconnected. Sill will reconnect when it can reach it."),
  and the automatic reconnect held in `reconnectAtReturn` until `cameForward`, which makes it `reconnect`
  with its loss counted from then. So the return is as after a trip the Mac ended while iOS had Sill
  suspended, whose loss is seen only on the return: the row dialed at once at home, and away from home the
  remote dials as after any loss, their 120 s counted from the return. Started at the close, a remote dial could connect in the
  background (only a row's dials wait for the foreground) and its 120 s could run out while the device is
  locked. The session's end withdraws the offer (the hub looks when `connected` changes), and with it the
  audio session's hold.
- No wire change: a device closing its connection is what every Mac already handles.
