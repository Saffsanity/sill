**The app icon in Liquid Glass, and a choice of icon with the community's (2026-11-18, branch `app-icons` from main at 8dbbb50, through `settings-pane`; draft PR #390).**

- **Noah, 2026-11-18**: "instead of merging https://github.com/Saffsanity/sill/pull/386/changes, I want you to take the
  original Sill icon and convert it to an Icon Composer document so the system draws it in Liquid Glass. Also, add a an
  app icon switcher in the version settings that is a full pane (being worked on right now) that allows the user to
  either 1) use the updated icon 2) use the one provided in this commit under community 3) generate a Genmoji or use an
  emoji and 4) upload their own .svg or icon composer document." Built here: 1 and 2. Not built: 3 and 4, since iOS
  changes an app's icon only to one compiled into it (docs/app-icons-plan.md says why, and what is left room for).
- **Made from `settings-pane`** (the whole-screen Settings, a draft by another branch) because its pane was on origin
  with this device's part in place; the App Icon group goes there, after Take the Tour, above the version. The branch
  merges `settings-pane` as it moves; its pull request's base is `settings-pane`, so GitHub shows only this work.
- **A, the original in Liquid Glass** (`Packaging/AppIcon.icon`): `design/AppIcon.svg` at its own coordinates, the
  gradient as the document's fill, two groups (the iPad: window, screen and camera, the slate body as glass; the iMac:
  screen, the body with neck and foot as glass), the original's gloss in the layers' fills (colours sampled within a
  few levels of the flat icon), its rims and shadows left to the system, translucency off, a lighter iMac body for
  Clear and Tinted. Both apps' primary icon: make-app.sh already compiled the document when it exists (comments only);
  the iOS project takes it as a resource in place of the flat `AppIcon.appiconset`, #386's wiring. `design/AppIcon.svg`
  and `site/icon.svg` stay flat.
- **B, the community's** (`Packaging/AppIconCommunity.icon`): grub-basket's document from #386 (be5113f1), byte for
  byte, in a commit authored by them; nothing else of #386.
- **C, the switcher**: `AppIconChoice` (pure; `Tests/checks/app-icon`, 50 mutants, 18 of them of the wiring in a copy
  of the files the check reads) and `AppIconPicker` (Settings' App Icon group: the pictures, the one shown ringed and
  checked, `setAlternateIconName` on a tap, iOS's alert, an orange line if iOS says no). The project declares
  AppIconCommunity in `ASSETCATALOG_COMPILER_ALTERNATE_APPICON_NAMES` (both configurations); the built simulator app's
  Info.plist has `CFBundleAlternateIcons` for iPhone and iPad and its Assets.car both icons' stacks and Any, Dark and
  Tintable pictures. `Scripts/app-icon-previews.sh` renders the pictures (and every look, `--looks DIR`). Sill for Mac
  keeps the original alone.
- **Cost**: the iOS app's Assets.car is 10.1 MB (both idioms) against main's 0.58 MB: about 5 MB a device after
  thinning, 2.4 MB of it the community icon. It is what actool makes of any Icon Composer icon: the same with an iOS 26
  deployment target, which keeps the 1024-pixel pictures too.
- **DEBUG**: `-SillAppIcon original|community|<name>` (a stand-in for iOS's name), `-SillAppIconFailed 1`,
  `-SillAppIconSet original|community` (a tap's real call, for photos of iOS's change), `-SillSettingsScroll appicon`;
  `Scripts/harness-photos/app-icons-duo.cases` and `app-icons-ipad.cases`.
- **Verified**: ictool renders of every look of both documents beside the original (in Noah's handoff folder,
  `next/app-icons/renders`); actool compiles both, alone and with the alternate, for iphoneos 17.0 and macosx 14.0
  without a warning; CI's build locally through heavy.sh (`swift build -c release`, `Tests/checks/run-all.sh`, the iOS
  app for the generic simulator, the CLI's refusal); harness photos on the agents' Duo and iPad against
  `settings-pane`.
- **Untested, for Noah** (docs/app-icons-plan.md, "What only Noah can try"): the icon on a real device in every Home
  Screen style, a change and its alert, iOS 17 or 18's fallbacks, Sill for Mac's icon in Finder and the disk image,
  App Store Connect's acceptance of the alternate icon on a TestFlight upload; the review notes' sentence and the
  comment on #386, both proposed in the plan.
