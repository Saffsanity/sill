**Home pacing: prevent forced-keyframe livelock on Personal Hotspot and congested Wi-Fi (#442; 2026-11-26, branch `fix-home-keyframe-storm` from main at 22ab5a3).**
When a Mac joins an iPhone's Personal Hotspot directly over Wi-Fi, Bonjour connects over link-local IPv6 (`fe80::...%en0`) and `StreamServer` serves the session as a home client (`!client.route.isRemote`). On 2026-11-25 (`Sill.log` 17:48:02 to 17:52:22, iPhone Duo `iPhone19,4` on its own 5 GHz Wi-Fi 6 hotspot, streaming `1512x982` at 60 fps and Low 4 Mbps), both the initial 192-icon catalog burst (`inflight` ~230) and a brief mid-stream scroll pause pushed `client.inflight > 2` and trapped the stream in a self-sustaining 28-keyframes-per-second livelock (`enc.out 56  net.dropped 28  net.sent 28` every second, zero `net.waitKey`, 5.8 MB queued, `rtt` climbing to `12539/12579 ms`, blurry rate-starved keyframes, and `"The link to wangnov can't keep up"` until the 4 s drain eviction dropped the session).

- **The cause (`StreamServer.broadcast`, home branch).**
  1. Dropping a delta at `client.inflight > 2` set `client.needsKeyframe = true` and immediately fired `wantKeyframe = true` (`HEVCEncoder.requestKeyframe`) while the backlog that caused the drop was still queued, with no spacing between requests.
  2. Sixteen milliseconds later, `if client.needsKeyframe` accepted the forced keyframe unconditionally without checking `inflight` or `pendingBytes` (and `else if client.inflight > 2 && !message.isKeyframe` likewise exempted periodic keyframes), while `broadcast(.parameterSets)` also sent parameter sets ahead of `decide` (`inflight += 3` per recovery keyframe). Each forced keyframe grew the queue further, so the next delta dropped and forced another keyframe on every alternate frame.
  3. Even on an empty queue, a large Retina keyframe (`0.5` to `2.5 MB`) that exceeds `SO_SNDBUF` stays in `NWConnection` across several frame intervals on a 20 to 40 Mbps hotspot or at 120 fps, so counting it against `inflight > 2` drops the third follower delta right behind it.
- **The fix: `HomePacing`** (`Sources/SillHost/HomePacing.swift`, pure, Foundation only; `Tests/checks/home-pacing`; wired into `Sources/SillHost/StreamServer.swift`, `Scripts/pacing/build.sh`, `Scripts/audio/build.sh`, and `docs/LAYOUT.md`). No wire change, no new setting, no new log line or `Stats` key, and `paceRemote` is untouched:
  - Steady state keeps `maxInflight = 2` (`inflight > 2` drops deltas rather than building a standing queue).
  - Parameter sets go out paired with each admitted home keyframe (`sendParameterSets: true`), never ahead of `decide` (so they never raise `inflight` past `maxInflight` before `decide` or flush the display when a keyframe is dropped). Only the first keyframe of a connection or restarted stream (`awaitingFirstKeyframe`) bypasses backpressure; later recovery keyframes require `keyframesInFlight == 0`, `inflightFrames == 0`, and `pendingBytes <= backlogBudget` (256 KB).
  - Dropping a frame sets `needsKeyframe` without asking the encoder while backed up. A recovery keyframe is requested once `inflightFrames == 0` and `pendingBytes <= idleBytes` (32 KB, above `audioCapHome`'s ~19 KB so live audio never starves recovery while catalog bursts and stalled frames drain first), spaced by `keyframeSpacing` (0.25 s, monotonic uptime) and waiting up to `inFlightKeyframeWait` (4.0 s, matching `deadAfter`) while another home client is still taking the previous keyframe so two slow home clients on a still window converge without ping-ponging, in both `broadcast` and the 1 s `sweep` (retrying if an earlier request went unanswered).
  - While a keyframe is still inside `NWConnection` (`keyframesInFlight > 0`), follower deltas pass up to `holdCap` (512 KB) beyond in-flight keyframes without ever stacking a second non-first keyframe; `backlogFloor` captures the remaining follower bytes when the last in-flight keyframe finishes and follows `pendingBytes` down on every completion (`bytesTaken`) and frame (`decide`) until `0`, where `inflight > 2` governs again.
  - Home 4 s drain eviction (`evictIfNotDraining`, after the 8 s connect grace) runs in `sweep` as well as `broadcast` and tracks non-frame bytes blocking a recovery keyframe, refreshing on any completion progress and clearing at `0`.
