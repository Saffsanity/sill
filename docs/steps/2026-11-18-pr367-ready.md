- What #367 does (kept): an app's sound is built on Sill's virtual display while the streamed window is staged there
  (`StreamCoordinator.appAudioDisplay`), not on the main display, where the app may have no window ("Start stream fail" in
  #359's log, 0.3 s after Sill's display was listed); the display's ID is part of `AudioKey.app`, so Sill's display made
  again (a frame rate change, a larger window, its loss) starts the sound on the new one at the select's end instead of 10
  s later; and the pipeline tries a source that failed or ended again by itself (`retryIn`, `scheduleRetry`).
- Fault 1, fixed here (#367's own, found by reading): its retry called `follow` again with the key that had failed and the
  last `make`, whatever the coordinator wanted by then, and it ran during a `select`. A staged window's sound now ends
  whenever Sill's display goes (the Desktop picked, Virtual Display turned off, the display made again), likely while the
  select is still awaiting; with the capture older than 10 s the retry fired at once, so the old app's sound was started
  again on the main display (a new epoch, the devices' format) and only then replaced by the Desktop's, which waited
  behind that start (up to 2 s, plus a 2 s look for the app). Now the pipeline only says a retry is due
  (`AudioPipeline.onRetry`); the coordinator asks for the sound it wants then (`audioFollow`: the source, the display,
  Send Audio, the devices and the lock as they are), and not during a select, whose end asks anyway (`active`'s didSet,
  set on every path of `select`; the same rule `adopt` keeps for Send Audio). `lastMake` is gone. A source that ends while
  a transition is under way leaves the retry to `run`'s end.
- Fault 2, fixed here: the retry had no bound. A source that keeps failing (an app none of whose windows is on the display
  its filter is built on, say) was started every 10 s for the rest of the stream, two log lines each, and up to 4 s of
  ScreenCaptureKit calls each time, which every other request waited behind. `AudioSourceRule.retryIn` now waits 10 s
  after the start, then 20, 40, 80 and at most 160 s while the tries keep failing in a row (`AudioSourceRule.failures`: a
  start that failed, or an end within 10 s of the start, is one more; an end after 10 s or more of running is the first of
  a new run; another source, or one stopped, starts at 0). A pick, a rotation or Send Audio off and on still tries at once
  once 10 s have passed (`decide`, unchanged). The plan's "No retry loop" (§4.5) now says why there is a bounded one.
