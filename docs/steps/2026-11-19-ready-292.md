- **What #292 does (addhumal).** `TouchHold` (pure): a finger held 0.45 s on the picture and lifted within 8 pt of
  where it was pressed right-clicks there, at the lift; moved on past 8 pt, the left button goes down where it was
  pressed, follows the finger and comes up where it lifts, so a window, a slider or a selection moves under it. A
  cancel or a three-finger stroke lets the button up and clicks nothing. InputOverlay's long press feeds it. The
  tour's hold row says the drag. The touch rig's `RIG_ONLY=hold` runs the real overlay's holds. No wire change: the
  drag is the left button's down, moves and up, as the Pencil's, which every Sill for Mac takes.
- **The switch, Hold, Then Drag (the review's row 2).** Settings › This iPad (This iPhone) › Hold, Then Drag, off
  unless turned on (`TouchHold.key` `Sill.holdThenDrag`, `TouchHold.defaultOn`), the device's own, never sent; read
  as each hold on the picture begins (`StreamClient.holdThenDragOn`, as Reverse Scrolling's is). Off, the hold is
  main's: the right click at 0.45 s, the finger's moves and lift dropped (`TouchHold.began(at:drags:)` returns the
  right click at once and holds nothing). On, it is #292's. The words under it are the review's: "Touch and hold a
  moment, then move your finger to drag what’s under it: a window, a slider or a selection. Lift without moving to
  right-click. Off, touch and hold right-clicks at once." `-Sill.holdThenDrag 1`, `-SillSettingsScroll holddrag`.
- **Where the row is.** Right before Reverse Scrolling, the first of the rows about touch: where #222's Touch Mode
  goes (draft PR #398), so with both the order is Touch Mode, then Hold, Then Drag. The review shows the row only
  under Direct Touch: whichever of the two merges second wraps it in `if TouchMode.stored(touchModeRaw) == .direct`
  (in Trackpad mode the pad over the picture takes the fingers and has its own hold, then drag, so the switch would do
  nothing there). The two pull requests meet at that one place in HostSettingsPanel.swift, and in its comment above
  the group (both edit it); everything else merges. Once the whole-screen Settings pane (`settings-pane`, draft PR
  #393) is in, the row moves into its This ‹device› part, the Touch and Zoom group the review proposes.
- **The tour's two forms.** `TourFeatures.holdThenDrag` (off unless set, so every other caller is unchanged):
  the picture card's hold row is main's ("Touch and hold to right-click.") while the switch is off and #292's ("Touch
  and hold to right-click, or hold, then drag, to move a window or select text.") while it is on: the same symbol and

- **The touch rig.** Its stand-in `StreamClient.holdThenDragOn` reads `RIG_HOLD` (run.sh passes it): off unless

  Hold, Then Drag on|off".
