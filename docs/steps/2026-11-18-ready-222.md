- **What #222 does (addhumal).** `TouchMode` (PointerPresence.swift): Direct Touch or Trackpad, `Sill.touchMode`,
  Direct Touch by default. In Trackpad mode the portrait trackpad's own surface (`TrackpadView`) lies over the
  picture in every layout (StreamScreen's and PortraitStreamScreen's picture panes), this device's own arrow shows
  (`TouchMode.showsPointer`), the zoom goes back to the whole picture when the mode changes, and the tour's picture
  card is "The Picture Is a Trackpad". The Settings panel's This iPad group has the row, a segmented control.
- **Finding 1, fixed: the pad over the picture could stop answering with Full Screen's controls hidden.** Its finger
  counter (`FingerCounter`, Q1's flip) only counts and never recognizes, takes direct touches and was not
  `WatchesTouchesOnly`, so `EdgeRecognizers.waits` made it wait for the swipe in; a watcher made to wait was never
  reset after the first stroke the swipe in held, nor the trackpad with it (docs/full-screen-plan.md: the touch
  rig's finding of 2026-11-12, on the stroke observer). Before #222 no trackpad was in the window while the swipe in
  listened. Confirmed by reading (the same three conditions the stroke observer met); it now only watches. The
  touch rig's new case shows it (below), not yet run.
- **Finding 3, fixed (the review's decision 4, its recommendation): the Apple Pencil and a connected trackpad or
  mouse stay absolute in Trackpad mode.** #222's surface took every kind of touch, so the Pencil was a finger on
  the trackpad and a trackpad's click landed at Sill's pointer, drifting from iPadOS's. `TouchMode.taker` (pure):
  only a finger is the pad's. The pad over the picture (`TrackpadSurface.overPicture`) gives its recognizers fingers
  alone, as the picture's own are, and hands the Pencil's touches and a pointer's clicks to the picture's input view
  (`InputOverlayView.takePointerTouch`, the path of its own touches, so they click and drag where they point); the
  events with no touch (iPadOS's pointer and the Pencil hovering, a trackpad's or a wheel's scroll) fall through to
  it, as #222 had them, and so does a trackpad's pinch or rotation, `.transform` (finding 2: harmless until #261's
  pinch, which then finds it). The on-screen trackpad is main's: every touch its own, nothing falling through.
- **A stroke the swipe in held carries its travel.** The pad's tracker waits for the swipe in, as the picture's pan
  does, so it began up to 8 pt late; it now counts from where the finger landed (`EdgeRecognizers.heldLanding`), as
  the picture's scroll does.
