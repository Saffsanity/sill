**Two-dimensional cursor scrubbing on the full key row's arrows and Space-bar floating cursor (2026-11-26, branch `feat/arrow-scrub` from main at 22ab5a3).**
Implements issue #438: sliding across the full key row's `[<] [v] [^] [>]` arrow caps or holding Space on the system software keyboard scrubs the Mac's text cursor in 2D without wire protocol or Mac host changes.

- **Pure state machine: `ArrowScrub`** (`iOSClient/ArrowScrub.swift`; `Tests/checks/arrow-scrub`, 21 assertions including 3,000 seeded random sessions verified against `KeyStrokes` and 24 mutants):
  - Quick tap (`< 8 pt` travel before `0.18 s`): emits one arrow press with any latched modifiers and spends the latch.
  - Stationary hold (`< 8 pt` travel at `0.18 s`): sends `KeyChord.down` and spends the latch once so the Mac repeats at its own rate until `end` or `cancel` sends `KeyChord.up`.
  - Slide (`>= 8 pt` travel): enters `.scrubbing` (releasing any held chord first and quantizing the crossing movement immediately), mapping horizontal travel to Left/Right presses with three-tier speed acceleration (`8 / 5 / 2.8 pt` at `< 240 / >= 240 / >= 520 pt/s`, guarded by a 120 Hz `minSampleInterval` floor) and vertical travel to Up/Down presses (`16 pt`) with cross-axis drift dampening and a combined 8-step per-update cap. Latched modifiers (`opt` for word jumps, `shift` for selection) stay active across the drag and spend once on lift or post-step cancellation.
- **Views.** On the `.full` non-scrolling key row (`PortraitStreamScreen`, open iPhone Duo and iPad, leaving the scrolling `.phone` strip untouched), `ArrowScrubCap.TouchView` tracks a single `UITouch` in window coordinates (`touchesBegan / Moved / Ended / Cancelled`, feeding final lift coordinates on `touchesEnded` so short vertical flicks are preserved and handling Control Center or Screen Recording interruptions cleanly). `InputOverlayView` connects `beginFloatingCursor` / `updateFloatingCursor` / `endFloatingCursor` to the same scrubber on every device layout.
- **Verified.** `Tests/checks/arrow-scrub/run.sh` (21 passed), `Tests/checks/arrow-scrub/run.sh --mutants` (24 of 24 mutants caught), `Tests/checks/key-strokes/run.sh` (505 passed), `Tests/checks/repo-rules/run.sh` (11 of 11), iOS Simulator Debug build, and live 2D scrubbing (including under active iOS Screen Recording) on an iPhone Duo (`iPhone19,4`, iOS 27.2.1).
