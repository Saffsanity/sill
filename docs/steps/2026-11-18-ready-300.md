The defects the evaluation found, each confirmed by reading before it was fixed:
- D1 (high): `HostClipboard` set a subscriber's images from every kind 32's own `accepts`, and a Paste
  carries none, so one Paste ended the Mac's images to that device for the session. Now
  `ClipboardPolicy.subscriberTakesImages`: a message that says `accepts` counts, a subscription (`{}`)
  is text alone, a Paste leaves it as it was. Model 4 of the check (3,000 sessions) fails with #300's rule.
- D2 (high): an image has no text, so `lockDecision` classed it a subscription, taken at any reading, and
  one sent while the Mac read locked was written after the unlock. Now `LockPolicy.clipboardRequest`:
  text that is not empty or an image is a set (`clipboardSet`, was `clipboardText`), refused if the Mac
  read locked at its arrival or now; a stop, which wins over anything with it, is taken.
- D3 (high): the host read a payload in one `receive`, so an 11 MB upload on a slow link was seconds
  without a header, and the remote door dropped the device as silent mid-Paste. Now `readPayload` reads
  it in pieces of 256 KiB, each a sign of life (`lastHeardAt`, `heard`), as the device's MessageReader.
- D4 (high): a copied image went like any message, counted in `inflight`, and frames queued behind it:
  the picture froze and the device was dropped as not draining (4 s at home). Now the image path
  (`StreamServer.sendClipboardImage`): beside the sound's, out of `inflight` and the drain eviction,
  counted in `pendingBytes`/`takenThisSecond`; it starts only while the connection holds at most 64 KiB
  and no keyframe crosses (`ClipboardPolicy.hostMaySendImage`, the files plan's `idleBytes`); the device's
  frames are withheld from the moment it waits until it has crossed, not counted against the link, and a
  keyframe brings the picture back (at once at home, as remote pacing asks away). The newest image
  replaces one not yet started. Since withheld frames never meet the home drain eviction, a device of
  either door silent 8 s while an image waits or crosses is dropped (`dropsSilentWhileImage`), and one
  that pings but takes nothing, once its image has waited or crossed 8 s and its bytes at 2 Mbit/s
  (`imageStalls`: 53 s for 8 MiB).
- D5 (medium): the receipt waited 4 s whatever the size. Now `receiptWait(worstRttMs:bytes:)` adds the
  bytes at 2 Mbit/s (8 MiB: 49 s at most), and a Paste over 256 KiB says "Sending to ‹Mac›…" until its
  receipt (`showsSending`).
- D6 and D9 (medium, low): `ClipboardImage.fitted` decoded without the orientation, encoded a photo as a
  PNG of tens of MB first, and its scaling context left transparency black. Now every re-encoding is
  ImageIO's thumbnail with the transform (upright, no tag), photographs (JPEG, HEIC, HEIF) go JPEG first,
  a PNG over its cap is not encoded as PNG again, and a JPEG of an image with transparency is drawn on
  white. Found here: ImageIO's JPEG encoder (macOS 27) already lays transparency on white; #300's black
  came from its own `noneSkipLast` context, which is gone. The white fill stays, and its colour is what
  the mutant pins.
- D7 (medium): the same 11 MB of JSON was decoded three times on the Mac's main actor and twice on the
  device's main thread, and encoded again to be measured. Now the coordinator decodes a kind 32 once for
  the lock and HostClipboard, the device decodes the Mac's once on a serial queue of its own
  (`clipboardDecoding`), sizes are read from the bytes, and a device's image is encoded again only when
  another device gets it. Measured here (`swiftc -O`, this Mac): an 8 MiB image's kind 32 is 11.36 MB of
  JSON (JSONEncoder writes base64's `/` as `\/`, within the 12 MiB cap), one decode 31 ms and one encode
  35 ms, so the Mac's main actor now spends about 31 ms on such a Paste where it spent about 130. It stays
  on the main actor: decoded elsewhere, the ⌘V that Paste on ‹Mac› Right Away sends after the receipt
  could be handled before the image is written.
- D8 (medium): the look read and fitted every copied image while anyone subscribed, devices on 0.6.2
  included, on the pasteboard's queue, where a device's text write waits with the main actor. Now the
  look reads an image only while Include images is on and a subscriber takes images, reads its bytes on
  the pasteboard's queue and fits them on `sill.clipboard.fit`; a fit that finishes after a newer copy,
  a lock or images off is dropped.
- D10 (low): 12 MiB was allowed to every connection's kind 32. Now
  `StreamMessage.maxClientPayload(for:clipboardImages:)`: only a connection whose subscription takes
  images while Include images is on (HostClipboard tells the server, `setClipboardImages`); the log line
  says the limit it applied. The word reaches the network queue a main-actor hop after the
  subscription is read, so a Paste of an image over 1 MiB sent within that hop of its device's first
  `{"accepts":2}` would be closed; a person's tap is hundreds of milliseconds after it.
- D11 (low): the words. "What a device sends with Paste still arrives", the chip's spoken words and the
  Paste hint say "text or image" while images are offered, "Couldn’t send the image you copied: it
  couldn’t be read." for an unreadable one (it said "Too big"), the site and README name the switches.

The switches (Noah's rule: a toggle wherever sensible; the evaluation's names, places, defaults, words):
- Include Images (device, `Sill.clipboardImages`), off unless turned on, in Settings › This iPad's Share
  Clipboard group under Paste on ‹Mac› Right Away, shown while Share Clipboard is on and the Mac says
  `clipboard` 2. On: the subscription says `{"accepts":2}` (a move of it subscribes again at once), the
  Paste control takes images, the chip lights for one, a Mac's image is written. The group's footnote
  adds its words. On this branch it is in today's Settings panel; the whole-screen Settings pane (draft
  #393, `settings-pane`) keeps this group as it is, so the row moves with it.
- Include images (Mac, `HostConfig.shareClipboardImages`, UserDefaults `shareClipboardImages`), on by
  default (the Mac owner's veto: the device's switch is the one opt-in), in Settings › General under Share
  clipboard with devices, indented, dimmed while that is off, its words under it. Off: the lists say 1,
  nothing of an image is read, sent or taken (`.refuse(.images)`). Applied at once (`setTarget`), named
  in `changes(to:)` ("clipboard images on → off"), never a device's to set. `-SillSetAfter '<s>
  shareClipboardImages=0'`; `-SillRenderPreviews` draws it on, off and dimmed.
- `SillHost --no-clipboard-images`: the lists say 1; without it stdout is as it was.

Decided here, for Noah to confirm (the evaluation's "For Noah"):
1. 8 MB at home: #300 cites a decision of his of 2026-11-02, of which no record was found.
2. The defaults: Include Images on the device off, Include images on the Mac on.
3. Away from home: (a), a smaller cap, 2 MiB (`ClipboardPolicy.awayImageBytes`), for both senders; the

   recommends; files, the camera and the microphone are paired-only.
5. #300's question: no re-encoding of a device's image on the Mac (its first bytes only; the app that
   pastes parses it).
6. Files on the clipboard: the files plan's later step, on #300's look.
